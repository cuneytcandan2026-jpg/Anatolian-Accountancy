Answers before you ask

Getting Started

It’s more straightforward than most people expect. Once you instruct us, we contact your current accountant directly for a professional handover: your records, filing history and HMRC authorisations. You don’t need to manage the transition yourself, and there’s no gap in compliance if you come to us with time to spare before your next deadline.

You’ll need to register the company with Companies House, set up a business bank account, register for corporation tax, and decide how you’ll pay yourself. We handle the formation itself and get the accounting groundwork (bookkeeping, payroll if needed, cloud software) set up properly from day one, so you’re not untangling things a year later.

It’s a conversation, not a sales pitch. Tell us about your business or personal situation, what’s not working with your current setup, and what you need help with. We’ll tell you honestly which services are relevant, roughly what it will involve, and next steps. No obligation to sign up on the spot.

Tax

As a sole trader you pay Income Tax and National Insurance on your profits, reported each year through a Self Assessment return. The part most people find confusing is working out what counts as an allowable expense and what doesn’t. We handle the calculation, tell you what you owe well before the deadline, and flag anything you’re entitled to claim but might otherwise miss.

It depends on your profit level, how you want to draw money out of the business, and what risk you’re carrying. There isn’t a single right answer. A limited company can be more tax-efficient once profits reach a certain point and gives you liability protection, but it comes with extra filing obligations. We’ll look at your actual numbers and talk you through the trade-offs before you decide anything.

Registration becomes compulsory once your taxable turnover passes the current VAT registration threshold set by HMRC over any rolling 12-month period, and you normally have 30 days to register from the point you cross it. Some businesses register voluntarily before that if it works in their favour. We monitor this for ongoing clients so it’s never a surprise, and can talk you through whether voluntary registration makes sense for you.

CIS

Under the Construction Industry Scheme, contractors deduct money from a subcontractor’s pay and send it to HMRC as an advance on their tax and National Insurance. We handle CIS registration, monthly returns and deduction statements for both sides of that relationship, and make sure subcontractors reclaim what they’re due rather than leaving it sitting with HMRC.

General

Most clients work with us entirely remotely, by phone, video call and cloud accounting software, regardless of where in the UK they’re based. If you’re nearby and would rather sit down in person, our Enfield office is there for that too. Either way, you deal with the same accountant throughout.

Yes, Bektaş speaks native Turkish and advanced English, and can support you in whichever language you’re more comfortable discussing your finances in. This applies to conversations, consultations and correspondence alike, not just a translated brochure.
