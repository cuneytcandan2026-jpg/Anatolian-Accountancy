---
question: How does CIS work for contractors and subcontractors?
order: 5
group: cis
---

Under the Construction Industry Scheme, contractors deduct money from a subcontractor's pay and send it to HMRC as an advance on their tax and National Insurance. We handle CIS registration, monthly returns and deduction statements for both sides of that relationship, and make sure subcontractors reclaim what they're due rather than leaving it sitting with HMRC.
