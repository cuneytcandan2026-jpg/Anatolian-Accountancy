---
question: What do I actually need to do to start a limited company?
order: 6
group: getting-started
---

You'll need to register the company with Companies House, set up a business bank account, register for corporation tax, and decide how you'll pay yourself. We handle the formation itself and get the accounting groundwork (bookkeeping, payroll if needed, cloud software) set up properly from day one, so you're not untangling things a year later.
