order: 2
group: tax

As a sole trader you pay Income Tax and National Insurance on your profits, reported each year through a Self Assessment return. The part most people find confusing is working out what counts as an allowable expense and what doesn't. We handle the calculation, tell you what you owe well before the deadline, and flag anything you're entitled to claim but might otherwise miss.
