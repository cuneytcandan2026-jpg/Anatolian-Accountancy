---
question: When do I need to register for VAT?
order: 4
group: tax
---

Registration becomes compulsory once your taxable turnover over any rolling 12-month period passes the current VAT registration threshold set by HMRC. You then have to register within 30 days of the end of the month in which you went over it. You also have to register if you expect your turnover in the next 30 days alone to go over the threshold. Some businesses register voluntarily before that if it works in their favour.
